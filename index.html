HomeCam Backup

HomeCam Backup is a small personal Android app for one household. It runs on a phone at home, records the household's own security cameras over the phone's Wi-Fi hotspot, and backs the recordings up to the owner's own Google Drive, so the footage is kept even if a camera or its memory card is lost or stolen.

It is not offered to the public, is not on the Play Store, and has no accounts, ads or analytics.

  • Records short video clips from the owner's cameras.
  • Uploads them to a HomeCamBackup folder in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Deletes old clips from that folder after a set number of days.
