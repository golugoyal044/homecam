HomeCam Backup — Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who uses the app

HomeCam Backup is a personal app used by one household on its own phone. It is not distributed to the public.

What data the app handles

  • Video and audio clips from the household's own cameras, recorded on the phone.
  • Camera login details (the cameras' local account), stored only on the phone.
  • A status file (status.json) with the app's health: camera states, battery temperature, pending uploads and recent app log lines.

Google user data

The app asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This lets it create files and folders in the signed-in user's Google Drive and manage only the files it created itself. It cannot see or change any other files in that Drive.

The app uses this permission only to:

  • upload the recorded clips and the status file to a HomeCamBackup folder;
  • check the Drive's free space; and
  • delete clips it uploaded once they are older than the retention period set in the app.

Google user data is not sent anywhere else, is not shared with anyone, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or to train any AI or machine-learning models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

Clips are kept temporarily on the phone until uploaded, then in the user's own Google Drive. The app has no server of its own; the developer receives no data from it.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. Clips in Google Drive can be deleted at any time from the HomeCamBackup folder, and the app's access can be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
